The Airedale Terrier, often called the "King of Terriers," is the largest of all terrier breeds. Known for their intelligence, versatility, and distinctive tan and black coat, Airedales were originally bred in Yorkshire, England to hunt otters and rats.

## Characteristics

- **Size**: Large terrier, typically 22-24 inches tall
- **Weight**: 50-70 pounds
- **Coat**: Dense, wiry outer coat with softer undercoat
- **Color**: Tan with black or grizzle saddle
- **Lifespan**: 10-13 years

## Temperament

Airedales are known for being:
- Intelligent and trainable
- Courageous and confident
- Friendly but alert watchdogs
- Energetic and playful
- Independent thinkers

## History

Developed in the Aire Valley of Yorkshire in the mid-19th century, these dogs were created by crossing the Old English Black and Tan Terrier with the Otterhound. They served with distinction in both World Wars, working as messengers, guard dogs, and search and rescue dogs.
