Beagles are cheerful, compact scent hounds originally bred for hunting small game. Their merry personalities and adorable hound dog looks have made them beloved family companions. With their big brown eyes, floppy ears, and ever-wagging tails, they possess a charm that's hard to resist. Beagles are known for their incredible noses, playful nature, and a gentle temperament that makes them wonderful with children.  While they can be a bit stubborn at times, their intelligence and boundless enthusiasm make them a joy to live with.
