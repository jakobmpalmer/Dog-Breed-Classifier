## Instructions for Creating Breed Files

1. **Research Sources**: Use Wikipedia, AKC (American Kennel Club), breed-specific clubs, and veterinary resources
2. **Length**: Keep each section to ONE paragraph (3-5 sentences). Total content should be 400-600 words
3. **Frontmatter Stats**:
   - Size: Height in inches
   - Weight: In pounds (lbs)
   - Lifespan: In years
   - Exercise: Low/Moderate/High
   - Trainability: Easy/Moderate/Challenging
   - Grooming: Low/Moderate/High
4. **Tone**: Professional, educational, but accessible to general readers
5. **File Naming**: Use kebab-case matching the URL (e.g., "golden-retriever.md")
6. **Image**: Place image as "image.jpg" in the breed folder
